Backpack Redesign: Create, dark steampunk

Sophisticated Backpacks as a leather pack: a stitched leather body with a canvas title label, the player slots in a pocket below (stepping in where the original does), a side pouch for upgrades, and light oak flaps for the tabs (raised, since they are controls; the oak of the inventory's workbench). The crafting arrow and result slot, buttons, switches and tank and battery gauges are the shared components. Both views are assembled with the mod's own cutting rules, so the art holds for every tier, the small-screen scrolling layout and an open tab. The scrollbar and the search box are drawn in code by the mod and cannot be restyled.
