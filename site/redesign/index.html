HUD Redesign: Create, dark steampunk

Dark steampunk on Create's parts: blackened iron, lit brass edges, and light only where something gives it (an amber lamp, glowing blood vials, roast drumsticks for food, porthole glass for air, liquid experience). Vector art at 8x resolution: Minecraft draws GUI sprites at their logical size whatever the texture resolution, so every sprite keeps vanilla's size and every item socket and icon cell sits where the game puts it. It can ship as a resource pack (sprite names below are the vanilla paths).
