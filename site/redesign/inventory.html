Inventory Redesign: Create, dark steampunk

The player inventory in the HUD's theme, keeping the spirit of the current panel: its scrolls become Create schematics (the armor column; the offhand and recipe book), its crafting table a workbench top (planks, an inlaid tray, a burnt-in arrow, an ember-lit result frame). Slots are thin sunken cells, empty armor and shield slots show dotted blueprint ghosts, and the Curios and Cosmetic Armor buttons sit where those mods put them. The title uses the game's font on light wood. Every slot is at its vanilla position; the panel ships as container/inventory (176x166 of 256x256).
