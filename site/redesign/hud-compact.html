Compact HUD: one action bar (concept)

Everything the action bar shows, gathered into one blackened iron bar instead of floating rows. The hotbar sits in the raised middle with experience as one glass pipe along its top edge, the level in a small window at its left end. The left wing is vitals: the offhand (a faint shield marks it when empty), health (absorption in gold) and food. The right wing is magic: Iron's mana and Ars mana, then our dash icon and count. Armor and breath appear only when the game would show them, extending the raised band sideways over their wing in a darker tone. Every meter is the same size, in ten segments, with the HUD's own icon; hover a meter for its value (current bright, max dim).

Every status the game shows here has a place (second row of buttons): heart effects recolour the health liquid (poison, wither, frozen), hardcore trims it in crimson, it shakes at 4 health or less, flashes on damage and carries a light sweep under Regeneration. The Hunger effect spoils the broth; holding food shows what it would restore (AppleSkin). Riding swaps food for the mount's health and a charging jump turns the XP pipe into the jump gauge, as vanilla swaps them. A charging spell (Iron's) runs a thin pipe over the sockets; the attack cooldown shades the selected socket; creative hides the vitals. A Create backtank shows alone in the breath segment, a darker copper meter with its time left always on it. Preview only: shipping it needs Create Dark Inventory to replace the HUD layers and draw this layout itself.
