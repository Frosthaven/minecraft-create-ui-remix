Compact HUD: one console (concept)

Everything the action bar shows, gathered into one blackened iron console instead of floating rows. The hotbar sits in the raised middle with experience as a glass pipe along its top edge and the level on a brass badge. The left wing holds the offhand, health (absorption in gold) with armor plating along its top, and Iron's mana. The right wing mirrors it: food with AppleSkin saturation on top, Ars mana, and the two dodge roll charges. Air rises out of the right wing only under water. Preview only: shipping it needs Create Dark Inventory to replace the HUD layers and draw this layout itself.
