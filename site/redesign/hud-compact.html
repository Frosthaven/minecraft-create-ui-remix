Compact HUD: one action bar (concept)

Everything the action bar shows, gathered into one blackened iron bar instead of floating rows. The hotbar sits in the raised middle with experience as one glass pipe along its top edge, the level in a small window at its left end. The left wing is vitals: the offhand (a faint shield marks it when empty), health (absorption in gold) and food. The right wing is magic: Iron's mana and Ars mana, then our dash icon and count. Armor and breath appear only when the game would show them, extending the raised band sideways over their wing in a darker tone. Every meter is the same size, in ten segments, with the HUD's own icon; hover a meter for its value (current bright, max dim).

Every status the game shows here has a place (second row of buttons): heart effects recolour the health liquid and the heart (poison, wither, frozen), hardcore trims it in crimson, it shakes at 4 health or less, flashes on damage and carries a light sweep under Regeneration. Saturation shows as the first food segments in cream (vanilla's yellow outline); the Hunger effect spoils the broth; holding food shows what it would restore (AppleSkin). Riding swaps food for the mount's health, as vanilla swaps them. A charging spell (Iron's), a camel's dash cooldown and a mount's jump charge take over the XP pipe, the level window showing their icon; the attack cooldown shades the selected socket; creative hides the vitals, spectator the whole bar. Health uses the real maximum (Max health 40), absorption continues past it in gold; left-handed players get the offhand on the right; a camel's dash cooldown refills the XP pipe in grey; AppleSkin's exhaustion is a hairline under the food glass. The held item's name (keys 1-9) and action bar text appear where vanilla draws them, clear of the bar. A Create backtank shows alone in the breath segment, a darker copper meter with its time left always on it. Preview only: shipping it needs Create Dark Inventory to replace the HUD layers and draw this layout itself.

Mods in this pack that draw at the hotbar or change its rows (read from their code; third row of buttons): Farmer's Delight Nourishment gilds the food broth and Comfort runs a slow sheen along health; the MekaSuit's energy joins the armor extension as a second pipe; an aircraft's health takes the right extension and its engine the XP pipe; a Create train's speed takes the XP pipe; the Helium Flamingo's charge takes the right extension; the steampunk armor's clock and compass and Artifacts cooldowns move to a small instrument plate above the bar (as vanilla places them they would cover the wings); Create's toolbox and schematic slot marks sit on the sockets, which keep vanilla's exact item positions.
