Compact HUD: one action bar (concept)

Everything the action bar shows, gathered into one blackened iron bar instead of floating rows. The hotbar sits in the raised middle with experience as one glass pipe along its top edge, the level in a small window at its left end. The left wing is vitals: the offhand (a faint shield marks it when empty), health (absorption in gold) and food. The right wing is magic: Iron's mana and Ars mana, then our dash icon and count. Armor and breath appear only when the game would show them, rising as raised ears above their wing: armor while you wear some, breath under water, with a Create backtank's air layered on top in copper while diving (it drains first; its time left always shows, as Create's counter does). Every meter is the same size, in ten segments, with the HUD's own icon; hover a meter for its value (current bright, max dim). Preview only: shipping it needs Create Dark Inventory to replace the HUD layers and draw this layout itself.
