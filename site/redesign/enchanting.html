Enchanting Redesign: Create, dark steampunk

The enchanting table as an arcane study: a dark oak desk, a bookshelf (bookshelves power enchanting), a candle, faint runes round the floating book, a leather book whose pages are the three options, an obsidian item slot and a lapis slot with a dotted ghost, and the player's inventory below, identical to every other screen's. Options are raised parchment pages (the game's brown text reads on them), violet when hovered (for its yellow text), dim and flat when they cannot be used. Layout read from the game's code; both views drawn by the same rules. Item art (the book, the item, lapis) is the game's and is shown as placeholders or left out.
