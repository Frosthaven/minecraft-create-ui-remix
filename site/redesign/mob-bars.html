Mob health bars (concept)

A flat, dim bar half a block above every mob within 10 blocks, facing the camera. The fill is the mob's health in its kind's colour; recent damage stays as a pale trail that drains away. Config (Create Dark Inventory): which kinds get bars (below) and the range (default 10 blocks).
